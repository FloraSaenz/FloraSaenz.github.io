Art & Animation

From traditional art to digital illustration, 3D modeling, and motion capture, my creative journey has always been about experimenting, learning, and bringing ideas to life.

I started drawing in third grade and stuck with traditional media for years. When I first tried digital art, I didn't enjoy it as much as I expected. Then March 2020 happened, and I found myself at home with a new iPad meant for college. I downloaded Procreate, and my life changed. Here are some of my favorite projects since then.

01 / DIGITAL ILLUSTRATION

Alebrije

This is the first digital piece I was truly proud of, and I still am. Inspired by Alebrijes, fantastical Mexican folk-art creatures, this piece pushed me to experiment with a non-human subject, full-color rendering, and a detailed background.

Focus: Digital painting · Character design

02 / CONCEPT ART

Nature Reclaims

This piece came from a freestyle assignment. I've always been drawn to stories where nature reclaims abandoned spaces, so I explored that idea with a broken-down robot tangled in weeds and grass. My favorite details are the rays of light, which I was drawing for the first time, and the robot's pose.

Focus: Environment storytelling · Lighting

03 / CHARACTER DESIGN

40 Original Characters

This assignment involved creating 40 completely original characters. It made me realize just how many character ideas I can come up with! Some concepts were more straightforward than others, but at least five have since found their way into ideas for other potential projects.

Focus: Ideation · Character development

04 / 3D SCULPTING

Kaiju Model

One of my favorite 3D models, this Kaiju was created in ZBrush for a modeling assignment. I remember my laptop fan going absolutely wild while I worked on it. The project took approximately 12 hours to complete, making it one of my more intensive sculpting sessions.

Tools: ZBrush · 3D sculpting

05 / 3D MODELING

Insect Anatomy Study

This ZBrush project focused on studying and modeling the body parts of an insect. It was a fascinating assignment that challenged me to pay attention to anatomical structure and detail. Having a reference helped, and the project took around five to six hours.

Tools: ZBrush · Anatomical reference

06 / MOTION CAPTURE

Motion Capture Experiment

This was one of my motion capture assignments. Some parts turned out rough, particularly the object intersections, limited finger movement, and lip-sync. Still, the project was a great learning experience.

I also recorded the voiceover at the last minute, whispering so I wouldn't wake my sleeping roommate. I edited the video and added the sound effects myself. Sometimes I miss those all-nighters!

Focus: Motion capture · Video editing · Sound design
