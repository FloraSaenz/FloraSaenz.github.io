Art & Animation

01 DIGITAL ILLUSTRATION

Alebrije

Inspired by Alebrijes, fantastical Mexican folk-art creatures, this piece pushed me to experiment with a non-human subject, full-color rendering, and a detailed background.

Tools: Procreate & Adobe Photoshop

02 DIGITAL ILLUSTRATION

Nature Reclaims

I've always been drawn to stories where nature reclaims abandoned spaces, so I explored that idea with a broken-down robot tangled in weeds and grass. My favorite details are the rays of light, which I was drawing for the first time, and the robot's pose.

Tools: Procreate & Adobe Photoshop

03 CHARACTER DESIGN

40 Original Characters

This assignment involved creating 40 completely original characters. It made me realize just how many character ideas I can come up with! Some concepts were more straightforward than others, but at least five have since found their way into ideas for other potential projects.

Tools: Procreate

04 3D SCULPTING

Kaiju Model

One of my favorite 3D models, this Kaiju was created in ZBrush for a modeling assignment. I remember my laptop fan going absolutely wild while I worked on it. The project took approximately 12 hours to complete, making it one of my more intensive sculpting sessions.

Tools: ZBrush

05 3D MODELING

Insect Anatomy Study

This ZBrush project focused on studying and modeling the body parts of an insect. It was a fascinating assignment that challenged me to pay attention to anatomical structure and detail. Having a reference helped, and the project took around five to six hours.

Tools: ZBrush & Autodesk Maya

06 MOTION CAPTURE

Motion Capture Experiment

This was one of my motion capture assignments. Some parts turned out rough, particularly the object intersections, limited finger movement, and lip-sync. Still, the project was a great learning experience.

I also recorded the voiceover whispering so I wouldn't wake my sleeping roommate. I edited the video and added the sound effects myself.

Tools: Maxon, Autodesk Maya, and Adobe Premiere

07 INDEPENDENT FILM

Adriftt

This short film was my senior project. For this project I was in charge of the concept art, the UV texturing, lighting, prop modeling, and served as an animation assistant for our two main animators. Over the span of a year, we worked hard to bring this product to life. I am very proud of this final product as it was the first time we had ever worked using every single step of the animation process.

Tools: Procreate, Autodesk Maya, Adobe Premiere, and Davinci Resolve
